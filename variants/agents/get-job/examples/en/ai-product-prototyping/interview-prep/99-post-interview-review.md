## Evidence-drift check

- [ ] Did I change Spring 2026, GPA 3.70, or another fixed education fact?
- [ ] Did I imply a Major GPA, honor, or course grade?
- [ ] Did I turn “locally runnable demo” into “shipped,” “launched,” or “production-ready”?
- [ ] Did I turn small-scope testing into a user count or research-at-scale claim?
- [ ] Did I imply that I wrote every line of code independently?
- [ ] Did I claim time savings, adoption, accuracy, or another result that was not measured?
- [ ] Did I describe a proposed next step as something already implemented?
- [ ] Did I treat an inferred interview stage as confirmed?

If any box is checked, correct the preparation material before the next interview. If the resume has already been submitted, prepare a direct clarification rather than a workaround story.

## Update rules

1. Add a new fact to [`../source-facts.md`](../source-facts.md) only when the real candidate confirms it and can explain its evidence.
2. Keep interviewer statements separate from verified company facts until they can be corroborated or clearly attributed.
3. Update the relevant bullet deep dive before adding a new standalone answer.
4. If a new stage is confirmed, add its source and confidence to `00-overview.md` before creating a stage-specific file.
5. Remove or qualify any resume claim that the candidate cannot defend; never solve an evidence gap with memorized wording alone.
