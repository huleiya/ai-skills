## Role target

The fictional JD describes an applied product role between product thinking and prototype delivery. It does not require algorithm research or production engineering ownership.

The five capabilities to target are:

1. **Workflow-to-requirement translation** — turn a real operating problem into a focused user flow and scope (`JD-1`).
2. **AI-assisted prototype delivery** — use AI coding tools to assemble and debug a working demo while retaining responsibility for decisions and validation (`JD-2`, `JD-6`).
3. **Structured product thinking** — define fields, states, transitions, error handling, and exports (`JD-3`).
4. **Testing and iteration** — observe confusion, identify the cause, and revise the flow or copy (`JD-4`).
5. **Clear product judgment** — explain tradeoffs, limitations, user risk, and a realistic next step (`JD-5`).

## Positioning decision

Use the target profile **AI product prototyping candidate**, not “software engineer” and not the ambiguous label “AI Coding candidate.” The resume should show a person who can frame a workflow, make product decisions, use AI assistance to build a testable demo, and explain what remains unresolved.

The ordering should be:

1. Target profile.
2. Education, unchanged from the source facts.
3. EventEase as the core project.
4. Experiences that explain the candidate's workflow observation, information organization, and communication skills.
5. Skills grouped around product decisions and AI-assisted prototyping.
