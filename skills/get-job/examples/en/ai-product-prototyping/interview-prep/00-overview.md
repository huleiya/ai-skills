# Interview Preparation Overview · AI Product Prototyping Intern

> Stage 3 overview for a fictional/composite role. The two stage-specific files are high-confidence inferences from the fictional JD, not a real company's confirmed interview sequence.

## The three things to remember

1. **Lead with EventEase as a product story.** The strongest evidence is the path from observed event friction to a focused workflow, structured fields, a working local demo, and two test-driven clarity fixes.
2. **State the AI boundary before it becomes a credibility problem.** Implementation was AI-assisted. The candidate was responsible for problem definition, scope, fields, test cases, acceptance criteria, and functional review.
3. **Treat limitations as product judgment.** The demo has no production deployment, scale metric, QR anti-sharing control, or advanced permissions. Naming those limits is stronger than pretending they do not exist.
